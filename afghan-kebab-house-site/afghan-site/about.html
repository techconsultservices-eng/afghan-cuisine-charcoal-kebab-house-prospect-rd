Our story

A taste of Afghanistan
on Prospect Road

"We cook the way it's always been done — over real charcoal, with patience and proper spice."

Afghan Cuisine and Charcoal Kebab House is located on Prospect Road in Blair Athol, Adelaide. We specialise in traditional Afghan cuisine, cooked over real charcoal for authentic, deep flavour that you won't find anywhere else in the area.

Our menu features signature charcoal-grilled kebabs — chicken, lamb, mince and mixed — as well as Kabuli Pulao (Afghanistan's beloved national rice dish), Manto dumplings, Biryani, and freshly baked Afghan bread made in-house each day.

Every dish is prepared from scratch using traditional Afghan recipes and techniques. The charcoal grill isn't a feature — it's the foundation. It's what gives our kebabs their characteristic smokiness and our meats their depth.

We're proud to have built a loyal following since opening, and humbled by our customers' generosity — rated 4.6 stars on Uber Eats with over 1,500 reviews.
